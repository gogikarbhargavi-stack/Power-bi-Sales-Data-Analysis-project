# Power BI Data Analysis Sales Project

An interactive, 5-page Power BI dashboard that analyzes sales performance
for 2020-2024. Every visual is connected to slicers, so you can filter by
date, customer, product, and promotion.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Overview**: Sales by city (map), total number of orders, average
   discount by promotion category, profit vs net sales, and sales trends
   by period.
2. **Top / Bottom 5 Analysis**: Best and worst 5 products by sales,
   quantity sold, and profit.
3. **Comparison**: Compare sales, profit, and quantity sold between any
   two date ranges chosen by the user.
4. **Edit Interaction**: Side-by-side date filters with controlled
   visual interactions.
5. **Table Visual**: Order-level details (sales, profit, discount, net
   sales) filterable by date, customer, product, and promotion.

## Key Business Questions Answered
- Which products perform best and worst?
- How do sales trend over time?
- What is the relationship between sales and profit?
- How do two periods compare?
- What is the average discount per promotion category?
- Which cities generate the most sales?

## Tools & Skills
- **Power BI Desktop**: report design and interactive visuals
- **DAX**: custom measures for net sales, profit, and period comparison
- **Slicers & Edit Interactions**: date, customer, product, and promotion filters
